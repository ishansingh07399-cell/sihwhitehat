# 🎤 SIH Presentation Script — Feasibility & Viability Slide
### ⏱️ Target: ~90 seconds

---

## 🟢 OPENING LINE
> *"Now let's talk about whether this solution is actually feasible and can work in the real world."*

---

## 1️⃣ FEASIBILITY *(20 sec)*
> *"Our solution is completely feasible because:*
> *AI and ML can read and analyze large, complex investigation documents — FIRs, CDRs, bank records — in just seconds.*
> *And the graph analysis in our system uncovers hidden and indirect relationships between suspects that a human analyst might miss entirely."*

---

## 2️⃣ CHALLENGES & RISKS *(20 sec)*
> *"Of course, there are challenges. The biggest one is that evidence data is fragmented — spread across multiple, disconnected systems. On top of that, information can be incomplete, inconsistent, or even deliberately misleading. Privacy and governance also need to be respected."*

---

## 3️⃣ OUR STRATEGIES *(25 sec)*
> *"To tackle this, we use four strategies:*
> *First — we bring all separate data sources together into one unified knowledge graph.*
> *Second — we resolve entities: same person, different spellings? We catch that.*
> *Third — every output shows a clear evidence trail, so the officer always knows WHY a connection exists.*
> *And fourth — a human-in-the-loop verification step ensures no AI mistake goes unchecked."*

---

## 4️⃣ LONG-TERM SUSTAINABILITY *(20 sec)*
> *"Finally, this is built to last:*
> *Officers can upload new evidence at any time — the system updates instantly.*
> *And our cross-case intelligence automatically links common phone numbers, bank accounts, or suspects across different cases — building a growing intelligence network over time."*

---

## 🔴 CLOSING LINE
> *"So in summary — it's technically feasible, strategically sound, and designed to scale with real police workflows. Thank you."*

---

## ⚡ QUICK TIPS FOR TOMORROW
- **Don't read** — glance at the slide, then speak naturally
- **Point at the slide** when mentioning each section
- **Slow down** on the strategies — that's the most important part
- If interrupted, say: *"Great question — let me finish this slide and I'll address that"*
- **Confidence > Perfection.** Judges respect clarity over jargon.
